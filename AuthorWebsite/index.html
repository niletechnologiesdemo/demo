About the Author

A life spent at the
edge of the page
and the water.

Meade Fischer is a California writer who has spent the better part of four decades on the coast — a freelance journalist, an outdoor essayist, and the author of eighteen books that move easily between travel writing, literary fiction, satire, and quiet spiritual inquiry.

His work has appeared in California Explorer Magazine, Bay Nature, Adventure Sports Journal, the Santa Cruz Sentinel, the Monterey County Weekly, Coast & Kayak, and on KUSP public radio, where he was a regular commentator. He wrote a weekly column for the Register Pajaronian from 2001 to 2005.

Whether tracing the shoreline from Santa Barbara to British Columbia in a sea kayak, sketching a Bigfoot novel in the redwoods, or arguing the meaning of an ordinary moment, Meade writes with the same instinct: the world is more interesting than we usually give it credit for, and the only way to find out is to keep going.
