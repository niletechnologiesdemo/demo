Two companies, one purpose

The shop exists so the shelter can

Kavanah Global began with a woman in a room in Queens saying she would be on a medication forever, and nobody having read her the leaflet that said otherwise.

Daisy Pearl started teaching that week. Then the classes needed a room, and the room needed rent, and the people who came to the classes sometimes needed somewhere to sleep. So there are two companies now: this one, which sells things, and kavanahglobal.org, which does the work.

You are on the first one. It is a real shop with real products that we would want in our own homes. It is also, structurally, the funding engine for the second — and we would rather tell you that plainly on the homepage than bury it in a footer.
