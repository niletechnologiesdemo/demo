Breakfast is not really about breakfast

A bed keeps someone off the street for a night. Breakfast is what makes them come back the next night, and the night after, until there is enough trust for a conversation about a programme.

That is the actual pipeline. Shelter is not the end of the work — it is the front door to it. Four hundred and twelve people who first came to us for a bed have since finished a wellness programme, and we count that as the number that matters most.
