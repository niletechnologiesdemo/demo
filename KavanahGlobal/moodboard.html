Taken from the crest

The purple is the velvet of the crown and the silk behind it. The gold runs from the deep shadow in the lion's mane to the highlight on the star. The cream is candle wax and the amber is the flame.

03 — Typography

A serif that answers the crest, a sans that gets out of the way

The wordmark on the crest is a high-contrast script. Cormorant Garamond is its closest workable relative — the same elegance, legible at any size. Inter carries every piece of interface text, where personality would only get in the way.

05 — Iconography

Drawn, never borrowed

A single stroke-based set at 1.6px on a 24px grid. No emoji anywhere in the product — a wellness platform dealing with addiction and grief cannot afford to look like a chat app.

06 — Photography

Warm, low, and never staged-happy

Candlelight, low sun, linen, worn wood, water, weather. People are shown in practice or in profile, never grinning at a camera. The palette of the photography is the palette of the crest: purple shadow, gold light, cream ground.
