Three things you will use every day

Mood Journal

Days and nights, written down

Two short check-ins — one in the morning, one at night. Mood, emotion tags, sleep, energy and craving. It takes ninety seconds and it turns character into weather.

After three weeks the insights view starts telling you things you did not know: that your worst hour is 4pm eleven days out of fourteen, that your mood tracks sleep more closely than it tracks anything else, that the days you write in the morning end better than the days you do not.

Open the Mood Journal
Community

A room with people in it, at midnight

Six circles, some open and some private to a programme. You choose a handle rather than a name — because nobody posts honestly about craving under their own.

Moderated by people who have done the programmes. Daisy answers in the threads herself, most days. The rule is simple: no advice unless it is asked for.

See the circles
Live sessions

Fourteen a week, and one at 6am

Every programme includes live sessions with Daisy — circles, classes, sits, kitchens and open Q&A. Sits run at 6am, 1pm and 9pm ET so there is always one you can make.

Most are recorded and stay in your library for ninety days. Closed circles — Freedom and After the Storm — are never recorded, by request of the people in them.

Kavanah Global Sanctuary

Land in the Hudson Valley, twenty-four rooms, and the thing that shelter and programmes together still cannot do: somewhere to stay while you rebuild.

Residential stays of two to twelve weeks, with your own room, meals, daily practice and the full Freedom programme delivered in person. Fourteen per cent of every dollar this organisation receives goes into building it.
