A person leaves a shelter at seven in the morning and goes back into the exact set of streets, rooms and people that put them there. We have watched it happen for eleven years and we have run out of patience with it.

The Sanctuary is twenty-four rooms on eleven acres in the Hudson Valley. Stays of two to twelve weeks. Your own room with a door that locks, three meals, daily practice, the full Freedom programme delivered in person, and land to walk on. No cost to the person staying — funded entirely by the shop, by members and by donors.
