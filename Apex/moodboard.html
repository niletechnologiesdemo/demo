UI Design Direction · v1

Bright, warm and quietly premium — a members' club that feels like daylight.

Apex sells access. The interface has to feel worth paying for without ever going dark, heavy or nightclub-ish. The premium signal comes from generous space, a restrained palette, a single serif used sparingly, and one disciplined gold accent reserved for the moments that matter — the deal, the unlock, the saving.

Light theme only. Every surface — mobile app, business portal, admin — sits on warm paper. No dark mode anywhere.
01 · Brand Architecture

Two pillars, one membership

The whole product reads through two named destinations. It gives the app a clean home screen, gives Sajjad a positioning line, and gives every business one place to post value and one place to post opportunity.

Apex Deals

Exclusive member offers from verified local businesses. Unlocked with a personal deal code, redeemed in store. Accented in gold.

Apex Network

Opportunities posted by the same businesses. Members register interest and share a profile; the business makes contact directly. Accented in harbour blue.

The Feed

Announcements from businesses a member follows. Keeps Apex a habit between purchases rather than a directory you visit once.

02 · Palette

Warm paper, deep forest, one gold

Forest green is the brand — it reads premium and trustworthy in light UI, and it deliberately avoids the sea of directory blues. Gold is rationed: it only ever appears on a deal, an unlock, or a saving. Harbour blue does the same job for Apex Network.

03 · Typography

Instrument Serif & Plus Jakarta Sans

A high-contrast serif carries the expensive feeling — but only on hero numbers, the savings figure and section titles. Everything functional is Plus Jakarta Sans, which stays crisp at 11px on a phone. Money and codes are always tabular.

06 · The Signature Moment

Locked, then revealed

This is the single most important interaction in the product. A free member sees the business, the offer headline and a blurred value behind a lock — enough to feel the loss, not enough to use it. The paid member gets the number, the code and a running quota. Everything about the treatment is designed to make the £10 feel obvious.

09 · Design Principles

What we hold to

01

Light, always

Warm paper on every surface, in every app, on every screen. Premium comes from restraint and space, never from a dark theme.

02

Gold is earned

The accent appears only on a deal, an unlock or a saving. If gold is everywhere it means nothing, and the paywall stops feeling special.

03

Show the value, hide the number

A free member should always be able to see what they're missing and never be able to use it. Blur, don't remove.

04

Savings are the hero

Every session reminds the member what the membership has returned. It's the strongest defence against cancellation we have.

05

No emoji, ever

All iconography is custom stroke SVG at a consistent 1.6–1.7 weight. It is the difference between a product and a prototype.

06

One system, three surfaces

Member app, business portal and admin share tokens, type and components. It reads as one company and it builds far faster.
